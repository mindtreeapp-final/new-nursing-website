Understanding CGFNS: An Essential Guide for International Nurses

April 14, 2026 Credential Verification 6 min read

For nurses aspiring to practice abroad, navigating the intricacies of licensure and certification is crucial. Among the essential steps in this journey is obtaining CGFNS (Commission on Graduates of Foreign Nursing Schools) certification. In this blog, we'll delve into the significance of CGFNS, its role in international nursing, and why it's a vital credential for nurses worldwide.

CGFNS certification is one of the most recognized credentials for internationally educated nurses seeking opportunities in the USA, New Zealand, Australia, and other countries.

What is CGFNS?

The Commission on Graduates of Foreign Nursing Schools (CGFNS) is a nonprofit organization based in the United States. Its primary mission is to evaluate and assess the credentials and qualifications of international nurses who wish to practice in the U.S. and other countries that recognize CGFNS certification.

Why is CGFNS Certification Important?

CGFNS certification serves several critical purposes:

  • Credential Evaluation: CGFNS evaluates the education, licensure, and registration credentials of international nurses to ensure they meet the standards required for licensure in the destination country.
  • Licensure Requirement: Many U.S. states and other countries mandate CGFNS certification as part of the licensure process for international nurses. It serves as evidence of their educational and professional qualifications.
  • Immigration Prerequisite: CGFNS certification may also be necessary for obtaining a visa or work permit in certain countries, particularly in the U.S.
  • Professional Mobility: Holding CGFNS certification enhances an international nurse’s professional mobility, allowing them to explore career opportunities in multiple countries that recognize CGFNS.

Important Update

Note: CGFNS has now transitioned to TruMerit for many international nursing pathways, especially for New Zealand and Australia. Mindtree Nursing Solutions provides complete support for both CGFNS and TruMerit processes.
