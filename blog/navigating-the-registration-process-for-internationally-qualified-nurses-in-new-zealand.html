Navigating the Registration Process for Internationally Qualified Nurses in New Zealand

April 14, 2026 New Zealand Registration 6 min read

Welcome back! In our previous blog, we explored the significance of the Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) and the initial registration process steps for internationally qualified nurses in New Zealand. Now, let's continue our journey by unravelling the remaining steps, addressing FAQs, and offering essential tips for success.

With the right guidance and resources, the NCNZ registration process becomes clear and achievable. Mindtree Nursing Solutions is here to support you every step of the way.

Competence Assessment

Some candidates may be required to undergo a competence assessment, which includes a theoretical examination and the OSCE. Successful completion of these assessments is necessary for registration.

Application and Fees

Submit your application for registration to the Nursing Council along with the required fees. CGFNS verification fees, Nursing Council processing fees, and additional costs apply.

Criminal Record Check

All applicants must undergo a criminal record check as part of the registration process to ensure fitness to practice.

Registration Timeline

The registration process varies in duration, depending on individual circumstances and the completion of required assessments and checks. Applicants can track their progress via the MyIQN online portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Withdrawal: Applicants can withdraw their application at any stage, but fees are non-refundable.
  • Enrolled Nurses: A dedicated registration pathway for Enrolled Nurses is under development.
  • Australian Nurses: Nurses with a current Australian practising certificate follow a separate application process.

Pro Tip from Mindtree

Stay organised, keep all documents ready, and track your application regularly through the MyIQN portal. Early preparation is the key to a smooth registration journey.

Navigating the registration process may seem complex, but with the right guidance and resources, you can successfully achieve your goal of becoming a registered nurse in New Zealand. Remember to stay informed, prepare diligently for assessments, and embrace the principles of culturally safe practice. Good luck on your journey towards registration and fulfilling your nursing aspirations in Aotearoa New Zealand!
