Specialised Services for CGFNS and Nursing Council Requirements

Navigating the credential verification process through TruMerit (formerly CGFNS) and subsequently meeting the requirements of the Nursing Council of New Zealand (NCNZ) or the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA) is a complex, multi-stage journey. Mindtree's consultants have in-depth, up-to-date knowledge of both processes and provide step-by-step support including:

  • Reviewing and preparing all required documentation
  • Coordinating with nursing colleges and licensing authorities on your behalf
  • Monitoring application progress and providing timely updates
  • Advising on how to respond to requests from verification bodies
  • Guiding nurses through IQN (Internationally Qualified Nurse) assessment processes

 Expert Knowledge, Fewer Mistakes

Even a minor error in documentation can delay your application by months. Mindtree's experienced team reviews every form and document carefully, so your application moves forward without unnecessary hold-ups.

Free OET and OSCE Training — A Rare Advantage

Professional exam coaching is expensive. Many nurses spend significant sums on OET and OSCE preparation, only to find that the courses are not tailored to their specific needs. Mindtree Nursing Solution removes this barrier entirely by offering free OET and OSCE training as part of their support package.

OET (Occupational English Test) Coaching

Mindtree's OET coaching is specifically designed for nursing professionals. Lessons are built around real clinical scenarios, helping nurses practise the exact type of language and communication that appears in the test. The program covers all four sub-tests — Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — with targeted practice materials and expert feedback.

OSCE (Objective Structured Clinical Examination) Training

The OSCE is a practical examination that assesses clinical nursing skills and patient communication. Mindtree's OSCE training is conducted in a simulated clinical environment, closely mirroring the actual examination. Trainers provide detailed feedback after each practice session, helping nurses identify and address areas for improvement before the real exam.

The free OSCE training at Mindtree was more thorough than paid programmes I had tried before. The trainers were knowledgeable and constructive, and by the time I sat the real exam, I felt completely prepared.
