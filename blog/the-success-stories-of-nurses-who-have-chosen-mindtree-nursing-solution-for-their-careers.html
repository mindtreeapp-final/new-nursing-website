The Success Stories of Nurses Who Have Chosen Mindtree Nursing Solution for Their Careers

April 14, 2026  |  Success Stories  |  5 min read

Mindtree Nursing Solution has witnessed numerous success stories of nurses who have chosen their services for their careers. From securing prestigious job placements in countries like New Zealand and Australia to successfully navigating the CGFNS and nursing council processes, nurses have achieved their goals with Mindtree's expert support. The personalised guidance, access to global study opportunities, and comprehensive training programs have empowered nurses to excel in their professions.

These success stories highlight Mindtree Nursing Solution's commitment to fostering the growth and success of nurses, making it a trusted choice for those seeking a fulfilling and rewarding career in the field of nursing.

Why Nurses Trust Mindtree Nursing Solution

For internationally qualified nurses — especially those from India — the path to working abroad can feel overwhelming. Complex paperwork, credential verification processes, English language tests, and registration requirements can discourage even the most determined candidates. Mindtree Nursing Solution was founded precisely to bridge this gap.

Over the years, Mindtree has built a reputation as one of the most dependable nursing career consultancies for Indian nurses aspiring to work in New Zealand, Australia, and other English-speaking countries. The results speak for themselves through the stories of nurses who have transformed their lives with Mindtree's support.

From Kerala to New Zealand

"I had no idea where to start with the NCNZ registration process. Mindtree guided me through every step — from my TruMerit application to my OET preparation. Within a year, I was working as a Registered Nurse in Auckland."

Key Areas Where Mindtree Has Made a Difference

1. Navigating the CGFNS / TruMerit Credential Verification Process

One of the most daunting steps for any internationally qualified nurse is completing the credential verification process through TruMerit (formerly CGFNS). Mindtree's team of experienced consultants has helped hundreds of nurses successfully compile and submit their documentation, coordinate with licensing authorities, and avoid common errors that cause delays.

 What Mindtree Offers for Credential Verification

  • Step-by-step guidance on TruMerit / CGFNS application
  • Document checklist and review support
  • Coordination with nursing councils and educational institutions
  • Application tracking assistance

2. OET Coaching and English Language Preparation

A strong English language score is essential for registration in New Zealand and Australia. Mindtree's OET (Occupational English Test) coaching program has helped countless nurses achieve the required scores. The program is specifically tailored for healthcare professionals, with a focus on real clinical scenarios, medical vocabulary, and exam strategies.

OET Success Story

"I failed OET twice on my own. After joining Mindtree's coaching programme, I passed all four components in one attempt. Their practice sessions and feedback were incredibly targeted and effective."

3. OSCE Preparation for New Zealand Registration

The Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) is a critical step for nurses seeking registration with the Nursing Council of New Zealand. Mindtree's OSCE training program provides hands-on preparation in a simulated clinical environment, equipping nurses with the confidence and skills to pass on their first attempt.

4. Personalised Career Guidance and Job Placement Support

Beyond examinations and paperwork, Mindtree offers personalised career counselling to help nurses identify the right opportunities for their skills and aspirations. Whether it is aged care, hospital nursing, or community health roles in New Zealand or Australia, Mindtree's consultants work closely with each nurse to match them to the right pathway.

5. Interview Assistance for International Nursing Roles

Securing a nursing job abroad requires more than qualifications — it demands confidence, communication skills, and an understanding of the healthcare culture in the destination country. Mindtree's interview assistance program prepares nurses through mock interviews, feedback sessions, and guidance on presenting their experience effectively to international employers.

Landing a Job in Australia

"Mindtree's interview preparation was unlike anything I had experienced before. They helped me understand exactly what Australian employers look for and how to communicate my clinical experience confidently. I got the job offer within two weeks of my interview."

The Mindtree Advantage: What Sets It Apart

Many nurses who come to Mindtree have previously struggled with the complex registration process on their own or received inadequate support from other consultancies. What makes Mindtree different is the combination of end-to-end support, a team with deep knowledge of international nursing registration systems, and a genuine commitment to each nurse's success.

  • Personalised guidance tailored to each nurse's background and goals
  • Experienced consultants with specialist knowledge of NCNZ, AHPRA, TruMerit, and IQN processes
  • Comprehensive training programs covering OET, OSCE, IQN, and therapeutic communication
  • Access to global opportunities through Mindtree's international employer networks
  • Ongoing support even after placement, ensuring a smooth transition abroad

Every nurse who has succeeded with Mindtree began exactly where you are now — with a dream and a desire for a better future. The process may seem complex, but with the right guidance, it is entirely achievable. Mindtree Nursing Solution is here to walk with you every step of the way, from your first enquiry to your first day of work abroad.
