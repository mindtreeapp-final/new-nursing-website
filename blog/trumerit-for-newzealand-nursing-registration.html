Introduction to TruMerit (Formerly CGFNS): Your Gateway to Nursing in New Zealand

April 8, 2026 | Credential Verification

If you are an internationally qualified nurse dreaming of building your career in New Zealand, understanding the credential verification process is your first and most important step. TruMerit (formerly known as CGFNS – Commission on Graduates of Foreign Nursing Schools) is a globally recognized organization based in the United States that provides credential verification services for healthcare professionals. In 2025, CGFNS rebranded to TruMerit to expand its support beyond nursing and serve a wider range of healthcare professions worldwide.

Why TruMerit is Important for New Zealand Nursing Registration

If you are planning to register with the Nursing Council of New Zealand, you cannot apply directly as an internationally qualified nurse. Instead, you must first complete a credential verification process through TruMerit. This process ensures that:

  • Your education meets New Zealand standards
  • Your license is valid and authentic
  • Your experience is verified

This is a mandatory and secure pathway for all internationally qualified nurses (except those registered in Australia).

Who Needs to Apply?

You must go through TruMerit if you are:

  • A nurse educated outside New Zealand
  • Holding a valid nursing license
  • Planning to work as a Registered Nurse in New Zealand

Eligibility Requirements for NCNZ

Before starting your application, make sure you meet the following criteria:

1. Educational Qualification

  • Diploma in Nursing OR
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (B.Sc.)

Your qualification will be evaluated by TruMerit for equivalency.

2. Work Experience

  • Minimum 1,800 hours of post-registration experience as a registered nurse in the last 10 years.

3. Active Nursing License

  • You must hold a valid and active nursing registration

4. English Language Proficiency

Applicants must demonstrate English language proficiency through either the OET (Occupational English Test) or the IELTS (International English Language Testing System) Academic.

OET Requirements:

  • Minimum score of 350 in Reading, Listening, and Speaking
  • Minimum score of 300 in Writing

IELTS Academic Requirements:

  • Minimum score of 7.0 in Reading, Listening, and Speaking
  • Minimum score of 6.5 in Writing

Candidates are permitted to achieve the required scores across up to four test sittings, provided that all required scores are attained within 12 months from the date of the first test sitting.

If you currently hold nursing registration in countries such as the United Kingdom, Ireland, Canada, or the United States—where English language proficiency was a requirement for licensure—you may be eligible to apply for an English language waiver through the Nursing Council of New Zealand. The council will carefully review your application and supporting documents to determine eligibility for the waiver.

Step-by-Step TruMerit Application Process

Step 1: Create Your Application

Register with TruMerit and start your application online.

Step 2: Purchase Your Order

  • Initial application: USD 380
  • Reactivation (if expired): USD 155

Step 3: Receive Verification Forms

After creating your application with TruMerit, you are required to purchase the appropriate service order through the portal. Once the order is confirmed, you will receive the necessary verification forms, which include:

  • Verification of your nursing qualification
  • Verification of your professional nursing license

These forms must be duly completed and sent directly to TruMerit by the respective authorities, such as your educational institution and licensing body.

Step 4: Submit Documents

Send completed forms directly from:

  • Your nursing college
  • Your licensing authority

Step 5: Credential Verification

TruMerit verifies and authenticates your documents.

Processing Time
The average processing time is:

  • 3 to 5 months

However, delays may occur depending on:

  • Nursing councils
  • Educational institutions
  • Document accuracy

Final Step: Apply to NCNZ
Once your documents are verified, TruMerit will issue a Credentials Verification Report. You can then submit this report to the Nursing Council of New Zealand and proceed with your registration.

Important Notes

  • Your application is valid for 1 year
  • If not completed, you must reactivate your order
  • Even small documentation errors can lead to delays or rejection
